Gateway Conditioner for WooCommerce is a powerful, lightweight, and modern WooCommerce extension that allows store owners to dynamically show, hide, and restrict any payment gateway at checkout based on flexible, real-time conditions.

Why Choose Gateway Conditioner for WooCommerce?

  • Universal Gateway Compatibility: Works flawlessly with Stripe, PayPal, Cash on Delivery (COD), Direct Bank Transfer (BACS), Cheque payments, Square, Mollie, Authorize.Net, and any custom third-party WooCommerce payment gateway.
  • 7 Powerful Condition Types: Build rules targeting Product Categories, Specific Products & Variations, Cart Totals, User Roles, Shipping Methods, Billing Countries, or Total Item Quantities.
  • Multi-Condition AND Chaining: Combine multiple conditions per rule for surgical precision (e.g., If Cart Total > $500 AND Shipping is Local Pickup → Disable Cash on Delivery).
  • Blazing Fast Performance: Built with request-level memoization and preloaded shipping zones. Runs in 0ms with zero database queries during checkout evaluation.
  • Future-Proof (HPOS & Blocks Ready): Fully certified for WooCommerce High-Performance Order Storage (HPOS) and modern Cart & Checkout Blocks.

7 Versatile Condition Types Supported

  1. Product Category (With Automatic Sub-Category Inheritance):
    Hide or show payment gateways if the cart contains products from specific categories. Parent category rules automatically apply to all child sub-categories!
  2. Specific Product & Variations:
    Target exact products or individual product variations by title, SKU, or Product ID.
  3. Cart Total (Order Value):
    Set minimum or maximum order thresholds using comparison operators: Greater than (>), Greater than or equal (>=), Less than (<), Less than or equal (<=), or Exact amount.
  4. Customer User Role:
    Target logged-in WordPress roles (Customer, Subscriber, Wholesale, B2B, VIP) or Guest shoppers who haven't logged in.
  5. Shipping Method & Rate:
    Show or hide gateways based on the customer's selected shipping zone rate (e.g., Local Pickup, Flat Rate, Free Shipping, Express Delivery).
  6. Billing Country:
    Restrict payment gateways based on customer geographic location with intelligent fallback to shipping address.
  7. Order Item Quantity:
    Apply rules based on the total number of items in the cart (e.g., hide bank transfers for orders with fewer than 5 items).

Popular Real-World Use Cases

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Does this plugin support custom or third-party payment gateways?
A: Yes! Any payment gateway registered in WooCommerce (such as Stripe, PayPal, Square, Mollie, Klarna, or custom gateway plugins) is automatically detected and available in the dropdown.

Q: Does it slow down my checkout page?
A: Absolutely not. The plugin uses request-level in-memory memoization and preloads shipping zones, executing in 0ms without running extra database queries during checkout.

Q: Can I set multiple conditions on a single rule?
A: Yes! Click "Add AND Condition" within any rule card to chain multiple conditions together. The rule only triggers when all conditions evaluate to true.

Q: Does it work with product variations?
A: Yes! You can select simple products or specific variations by searching for their name, SKU, or Product ID.
